# Writing and clarity guide

For every word on the Nobleman Productions website and client portal. It comes from the *Murphy's Laws on Technology* poster: each law below is a failure we design against. Check new copy against the rules before it ships.

## The rules

| # | Rule | The law it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Say it plainly.** Short sentences, everyday words. If a term needs explaining, use a plainer word instead, or explain it in the same sentence. | "Any simple theory will be worded in the most complicated way." · "If you can't understand it, it is intuitively obvious." |
| 2 | **Assume nobody reads instructions.** Every page says what it's for in one line, shows where you are, and makes the next step the most obvious thing on the screen. | "When all else fails, read the instructions." · "Build a system that even a fool can use…" |
| 3 | **Buttons say exactly what happens.** Use a verb and its object: "Approve Version 3", "Download the film". Not "Submit", "OK", "Continue". | "Give all orders verbally." (Be explicit about what you're asking someone to do.) |
| 4 | **Promise only what is always true.** No absolute guarantees about time, price, or results that one bad week could break. Say "usually", "typically", or "we aim to" where it's honest. State facts we can prove, and nothing we can't. | "Never write anything down that might go into a 'Pearl Harbor File.'" · "Nothing ever gets built on schedule or within budget." |
| 5 | **Never describe a feature that doesn't exist yet.** If it's a demo or simulated, say so. | "If it's not in the computer, it doesn't exist." |
| 6 | **Plan for the mistake.** Ask for confirmation before anything hard to undo. After any action, say what happened and what comes next. When something fails, say what went wrong and what to do about it. | "Computers are unreliable, but humans are even more unreliable." · "The one that will cause the most damage will be the one to go wrong." |
| 7 | **No dead ends.** An empty list says why it's empty and what to do. Every page has a way back. | "Fill what's empty. Empty what's full." |
| 8 | **Write for the busy decision-maker, not the technician.** Lead with what it means for them; put the technical detail second, if at all. | "The degree of technical competence is inversely proportional to the level of management." |
| 9 | **Proofread.** Spelling, names, dates, and directions ("below" must really be below). | "Work smarder and not harder and be careful of yor speling." |

## Plain-word glossary

Use the word on the right on anything a client sees.

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| cut, edit (as a noun) | version |
| cutdown | shorter version |
| deliverable, asset | finished film, file |
| timecode | this moment (the time shown, e.g. 0:42) |
| review copy | preview (watermarked) |
| finishing, finishing pass | final polish (colour and sound) |
| revision request | a list of changes |
| ProRes master | editing master (very large, for broadcast or future edits) |
| H.264 / MP4 web | ready for web and social |
| 9:16, 16:9 | vertical (phones), widescreen |
| RAW + JPEG | original camera files plus ready-to-use photos |
| camera originals | all the raw footage we filmed |
| pre-production | planning |
| DP | director of photography (camera lead) |
| V3 | Version 3 |

## Promises: say it this way

| Don't write | Write |
|---|---|
| A personal reply the same business day. | A personal reply, usually the same business day. |
| Same-day highlight reels. | Same-day highlight reels when planned into the shoot. |
| What you approve is what you're billed. | Your quote is in writing before work starts; nothing is added without your OK. |
| Uploads resume if interrupted. | (Only if the feature exists. Otherwise say nothing, or say it's coming.) |

Facts with evidence behind them stay as they are, for example the 50,000+ concurrent viewers or the 53 → 8,000 subscriber growth. Keep them exact and don't round them up.

## Checklist before shipping copy

- [ ] Could a first-time visitor say what this page is for, and what to do next, within five seconds?
- [ ] Does every button name its action?
- [ ] Is there any promise here that isn't true every single time?
- [ ] Does anything describe a feature that isn't real yet?
- [ ] Is there any jargon from the glossary's left-hand column?
- [ ] Spelling, names, dates, directions checked?
